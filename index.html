Please submit your RSVP via our website on or before 30 November 2026 so we can finalize catering and seating with the venue.

The ceremony begins promptly at 16:00. Please aim to arrive at Intiem Weddings around 15:30 so you have plenty of time to park and take your seats.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Due to venue capacity limits, we can only accommodate guests formally named on your wedding invitation. Thank you so much for understanding!
